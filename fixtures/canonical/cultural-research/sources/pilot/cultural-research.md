# Maru research — synthetic fixture evidence

Version: pilot-1. Captured: 2026-09-18. The studies and results below are
invented test evidence and must not be presented as real research findings.

## red-attention

In this invented 2025 trial, 40 adults recruited in Vietnam viewed software
notifications. Participants detected the red-bordered notification more often
than the otherwise identical gray-bordered notification. The measured result
was detection in this task; the study did not measure symbolism, preference,
safety, children, other countries, or a region-wide effect.

## blue-symbolism

This invented survey asked adults recruited in Thailand what blue packaging
symbolized to them. It contains neither a red-versus-gray comparison nor a
measure of attention.

## rouge-terms

“Rouge” appears as a French color term and as the name of a separate research
project in this collection. The terminology note defines both uses but reports
no effect of using red.

## earlier-pilot

An unreviewed proposal suggested testing red labels on toy packaging with
children in another country. No trial was performed and no results exist.
The proposal is retained as research history, not as an observed finding.
