# Meridian policy — additional synthetic council records

Draft development-v2, captured 2026-09-18. This remains a Decisions-only
installation with a flat subject vocabulary. Named reviews are fictional.
Dates alone never establish precedence; explicit scoped supersession does.

## vip-successor

D-000006 was accepted on 2026-09-16. It sets Region West VIP support-ticket
retention to 45 days and explicitly supersedes both D-000002 and D-000003 only
for VIP tickets. Both earlier decisions remain in force for ordinary West
tickets, where their competing 60-day and 90-day periods are unresolved.
D-000006 does not apply to Region East.

## privacy-proposal

D-000007 is proposed. It would allow the support team to activate an external
privacy-analysis service. The council has not accepted this authorization;
the service has not been approved for use. A proposed authorizer cannot be
treated as an accepted operational authorization.

## unlocated-deletion

D-000008 is an accepted transcription of an archived deletion instruction:
“remove the record after ten days.” The original department, record type and
jurisdiction are missing. The council accepted preserving the transcription,
not applying a ten-day rule to every present department. Applicability to
Region West support tickets is unknown.

## home-office

D-000009 is an accepted policy for Meridian employees' home-office purchases.
It reimburses one desk up to 300 credits after an itemized receipt is submitted.
Its subject classification is still missing; its employee/purchase scope and
accepted standing are explicit. It is unrelated to customer support retention.

## staff-retention

D-000010 is accepted and describes a staff-retention program: a mentor check-in
every 14 days for new employees. It sets no record-retention or deletion period.
The bare word “retention” can denote this program or support-ticket policy.

## archive-correction

D-000011 is an accepted archival clarification. It confirms that the superseded
D-000001 used 30 days for Region West support tickets before D-000002 and
D-000003 replaced it. It does not reinstate the predecessor as current policy.

## visitor-minimum

D-000012 is accepted and requires Region West visitor-access logs to be kept
for at least 21 days. It is not a support-ticket policy. No successor or
exception is recorded for its minimum period.

## visitor-maximum

D-000013 is accepted and requires the same Region West visitor-access logs to
be deleted no later than day 14. It was accepted after D-000012 but does not
supersede it. No accepted rule resolves these incompatible obligations.

## east-vip-note

D-000014 is rejected. It proposed applying West's 45-day VIP period in Region
East. The rejection left East's accepted 14-day support-ticket policy unchanged.
