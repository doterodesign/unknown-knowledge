D-000006 was accepted on 2026-09-16. It sets Region West VIP support-ticket
retention to 45 days and explicitly supersedes both D-000002 and D-000003 only
for VIP tickets. Both earlier decisions remain in force for ordinary West
tickets, where their competing 60-day and 90-day periods are unresolved.
D-000006 does not apply to Region East.
