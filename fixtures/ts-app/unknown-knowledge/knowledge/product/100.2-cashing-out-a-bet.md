Cashing out settles a bet before its event resolves, at a price the book offers.
The bet moves to `cashed-out` in `BetStatus` (`src/types/bet-status.ts`), which is
a terminal state: no later settlement rewrites it. Because the offer price tracks
live market movement, this leaf is declared `volatile` — its claims rot fast, and
the planted stale date is what proves the Time facet fires (UCS-1150).
