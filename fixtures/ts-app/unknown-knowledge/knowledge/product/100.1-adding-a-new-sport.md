Adding a sport is a one-line registry change plus the matching ontology update.
Add the sport's key to `SUPPORTED_SPORTS` in `src/registry/sports.ts`, then
update concept K-101's `enumerates.values` in the same PR — the value
validator diffs the two both ways and blocks on any mismatch.
