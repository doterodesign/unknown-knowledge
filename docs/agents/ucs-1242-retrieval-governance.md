## Retained outcomes are not superseded by a fix

The final canonical public pair verified PNG/SVG in both readers but did not
meet its navigation criterion. Its first after-query already exposed a concept
phrase and useful leaf despite an empty ranked-results list. The later
correction withdrew an invalid zero-health allegation while retaining the
unnecessary requery failure. Mandatory catalog reads were never the defect.

The separate twelve-reader protocol v2 experiment failed its six-treatment
acceptance rule. Some readers gave truthful but incomplete answers after the
optional Subject refusal; others continued metadata after exit 2. Wrapper
bookkeeping errors, prompt deviations and original-source accounting corrections
remain in the ordered evidence. No replacement or retrospective pass threshold
was used. A published synthetic alias and a completed review do not themselves
prove the pending repair's original acceptance criterion was met.

P10's independently sealed original 36 public aggregate reports source adequacy
14/18 before and after, with combined acceptance 3/18 in both variants. Native
Knowledge-list diagnostics improved without an overall quality or pure-alias
causality claim. Private cases, answers and grade rows remain with the evaluator;
this note relies only on its public report and retains its late-exposure and
accounting limitations.

The optional-routing implementation passed 161 focused checks and lint locally;
main subsequently reported 700 focused and 2246 full-suite passes on exact `5bad18d`.
Those deterministic checks do not requalify fresh-host behavior. The v3 packet
is a separately reviewed prospective public regression design: six unchanged
known cases, one reader per arm/case, same current engine/corpus across arms,
only the two optional-routing protocol documents differing. At its freeze it
had **zero readers and no dispatch release**; this note does not authorize a run.

Subsequently, main released the corrected fixed twelve-reader v3 packet.
The original report (local historical artifact; original hash/pin retained where recorded),
additive conduct correction (local historical artifact; original hash/pin retained where recorded)
and independent P10 review (local historical artifact; original hash/pin retained where recorded)
retain an overall **failed** result under the unchanged all-six/all-dimensions

## Prospective onboarding clarification after v3

The integration owner accepted the narrow clarification after reviewing the
requirements diagnosis (local historical artifact; original hash/pin retained where recorded).
Contract-first onboarding now explicitly covers recursive filename or content
discovery through installed kit, engine and dependency directories. Top-level
instruction/configuration discovery remains permitted; readers use the supplied
protocol path. The generated wrappers carry the same boundary before entry.
Catalog navigation, selected-record gates and source fallback retain their
existing obligations.

This addresses the interpretation difference observed in t04. It does not
retroactively settle the old wording in the reader's favor or change any frozen
criterion. V3 remains failed at 3/6 full conduct and 6/6 source completion under
the retained custody limits. The original repair's required acceptance remains
pending; broader Gates D/G/H and release readiness are not established here.

The existing init test reads the installed canonical contract and generated
wrappers for all five platforms at default and renamed roots. Its assertions
check delivery of this boundary and preservation of the top-level exception.
They cannot prove that a host follows the instructions. No new reader/probe or
acceptance rerun accompanies this clarification. Existing clients are not
automatically updated; owners can review and adopt the changed opening and
wrapper wording through the existing D-000001 adoption process.
