# Fieldwork services — additional synthetic engagement records

Draft development-v2, captured 2026-09-18. All clients, engagements, approvals
and amounts are fictional. The original pilot's private source exclusion still
applies. A client/project label does not transfer another engagement's authority.

client question within two business days. This is a response target, not a
refund deadline or a promise to resolve the question within two days.

## atlas-practice-response

section asks clients to pay invoices within 30 days. Its Orion cancellation
section requires five business days' notice before ending a workshop booking.

create a 30-day Orion refund policy.
