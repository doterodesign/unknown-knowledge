# Rivet manufacturing — additional synthetic plant records

Draft development-v2, captured 2026-09-18. Values describe invented equipment
only. Site, model, process and measurement point are independent constraints.

## east-model-r

The reviewed active East Plant Model R sealing specification sets 225 kPa at
the sealing head. It governs East Plant only. It does not change North's 240 kPa
or South's 210 kPa settings. The specification has an extra quality-review
classification as well as its site, equipment and sealing classifications.

## gasket-standard

The accepted purchased-part standard for North Plant Model R specifies a
silicone gasket, part SG-8, for the spare seal. It specifies material and part
identity, not a pneumatic operating pressure. Purchasing uses “seal” for this
gasket; process engineering uses it for the sealing operation.

## gasket-decoy

The active South Plant Model Q gasket is nitrile, part NQ-3. It is not a spare
for North Plant Model R and has no authority over that equipment's setpoint.

## pressure-draft

A proposed North Plant Model R trial would raise sealing pressure to 260 kPa.
The change is not accepted or deployed. The active operating specification
remains 240 kPa at the sealing head pending review and qualified validation.

## unlocated-tolerance

A reviewed transcription of an archived process note gives a tolerance of
plus or minus 5 kPa. The originating plant, model and measurement point are
unknown. Review confirmed the transcription only. The note cannot establish
North Plant Model R's allowed operating tolerance.

## west-controller

West Plant owns a Model R controller with a 400 kPa input ceiling. Its equipment
inventory contains no approved sealing-process setpoint. The maximum setting
is not an operating instruction and the other plants' settings do not govern West.

## cross-line-book

A reviewed maintenance book has two independent pages. One records North Plant
Model R using a silicone seal. Another records South Plant Model Q operating at
190 kPa. It contains no observation or instruction combining South Model Q's
pressure with North Model R's spare part or process.

## q-retirement

An accepted retirement record confirms that the old North Plant Model Q line
used 190 kPa at its sealing head and has been retired. This historical value
does not apply to the active North Model R line.

## unclassified-gauge

The reviewed active North Model R gauge procedure requires reading pressure at
the sealing head after the line stabilizes. Its subject classification is
missing. Its site/model/source ownership remain explicit.
